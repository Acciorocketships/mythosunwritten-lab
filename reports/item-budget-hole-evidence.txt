writable, and sim/item.gd now states that exactness is a guarantee of the
constructors and that anything writing an axis afterwards owns keeping the sum.

purpose, to show that spends_budget() is a live report; making the fields
read-only would have had to edit both, outside sim/item.gd and its two callers.
