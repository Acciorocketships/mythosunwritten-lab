The record of a blow: the same record whether a person struck it or a character
deciding for itself did.

Command:  ./run_strike.sh
Reproduces byte for byte in any process on this seed: the same command run twice
into two files diffs clean.
Taken at cycle 203, on the commit that widened the blow record.

Alder's turns are taken by hand through BoardTurn -- turn until the spear covers
somebody, swing, and end the turn a tick later. Briar has a decision function
that chooses attack and nothing else, serviced by ControlLoop and answered by
ActionEngine. Both spend their weapon action through CombatMatch.attack, which is
the one place a blow is written down.
