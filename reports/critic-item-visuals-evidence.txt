6. Two things found, both reproducible.
==============================================================
  (i)  A blow a person spends is drawn one tick after the tick the record
       names it began on; a model-driven character's is drawn on it.
       Reproduce: ./tools/critic_item_privilege_probe.sh, section "ranged".
       #1 (a person, through BoardTurn) is drawn swinging on ticks 11..36 --
       26 of the 27 the record promises -- and its arrow leaves at phase
       0.038 rather than 0.000.  #2 (its own decision function, through
       ActionEngine) is drawn 18..44, 27 of 27, leaving at phase 0.000.
       Cause: a person spends the weapon action outside the tick, so the row
       is stamped with the clock's current reading and only reaches
       ActionScene.blows at the next _take_blows() (sim/action_scene.gd:600),
       by which time the clock has moved on.  Nothing branches on who
       decided: the record's keys are identical and the drawing code is the
       same.  The earlier review already recorded the same one-tick offset one
       layer down ("the person pressing late", section F of
       tools/critic_privilege_probe.gd), so this is that known property's
       cost in the picture, not something this milestone introduced.
       Smallest change: drain the board at the end of a hand-spent action --
       call ActionScene._take_blows() where the hand path returns -- so a
       hand-spent blow reaches `blows` on the tick it names.

  (ii) A projectile blow that found nobody draws an arrow that never leaves
       the archer.  sim/combat_match.gd:285 writes to_cell as
       first.get("cell", me.cell), so a swing that hit nothing has
       to_cell == from_cell; render/blow_flights.gd copies both endpoints
       verbatim (correctly -- it must not second-guess the simulation), so
       the flight is zero-length and stands on the archer for the whole
       motion.  Reproduce: ./tools/critic_item_privilege_probe.sh, section
       "a blow that found nobody" -- 26 ticks (1.3 s at twenty ticks a
       second) of an arrow crossing 0 cells.  Only a person can produce it:
       BoardTurn.swing spends the action wherever the piece faces, while
       ActionEngine refuses an attack whose pattern does not cover the named
       target and CombatPolicy._swing only swings when covered > 0.
       Smallest change: in BlowFlights.flights(), do not make a flight for a
       row whose to_cell equals its from_cell.
