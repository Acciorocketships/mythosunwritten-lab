  counted nothing for it; the same refused sentence came back 39 times. After the

  Bram's 2 in the before column is not the lock and is worth saying plainly: a
  character that stops moving changes what everybody else sees, so Bram's third
  question stopped matching anything in the recording and it had no reply to give
  him (the `no reply' column). It is an artefact of replaying a recording against
  a world that has diverged from it, not of the bug -- and after the change,
  nobody's questions go unanswered.
