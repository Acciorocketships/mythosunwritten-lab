A person driving one of the cast: the shell trace, seed 1234.

This machine has no display, so the built shell was driven under xvfb with
the keys pressed from inside -- through Input.parse_input_event, which puts
them on the engine's own queue, so they arrive at the same _unhandled_input
binding a person's press does. The command:

  xvfb-run -a tools/godot/godot4 --path . --resolution 1280x720 --fixed-fps 60 -- \
      --seed 1234 --play --journal --sheet --input "2:w,24:g,46:j,52:k" \
      --screenshot reports/assets/player-input-refusal.png --screenshot-tick 62

W walks a step, G goes to the nearest named place, J hops 3.0 units, and K
leaps 12.0 -- further than DEX 3 reaches, so the engine refuses it. Every one
of the four is an action out of the same catalogue every other character
chooses from; render/player_controls.gd builds nothing but Action.go_to,
Action.go_to_offset and Action.jump, and the world's own ControlLoop commits
them.

W and G are the two shapes of one row. A key press is a direction and a
length and never a place, so W writes its step under `go_to`'s `offset` key --
`chose go_to(offset=(0.000, -3.600))` below -- and the engine works out where
that lands from where Pip is standing when the walk starts. G has somewhere in
mind and writes it under `target`. This is the same pair of shapes a language
model is offered, on the same row of the same catalogue; see
reports/position-space-evidence.txt.
