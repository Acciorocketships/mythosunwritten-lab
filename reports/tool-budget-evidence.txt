  no live call in it:

    	Percent of CPU this job got: 99%
    	Elapsed (wall clock) time (h:mm:ss or m:ss): 0:53.97
    = 18 ms a tick, against the 50 ms the world is stated to be stepped at.

  so the 35 minutes is a level and not a slope. A live run sleeps
  ScriptedAgent.TICK_MS = 50 ms a tick on purpose (bin/agent_main.gd,
  _pacing), which is 2.5 minutes of it; the rest is what a tick costs while
  calls are in flight -- 0.65 s a tick at 2.053 calls a tick, 0.57 s at
  1.003 -- which barely moved when the calls halved, so it is per-tick and
  not per-call, and it is in the live transport rather than in the world.
  Reported as a finding, not fixed here.
