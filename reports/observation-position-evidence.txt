why the second bargain pass was made, said plainly
  The first bargain draw of 2026-09-09 did not close the sale: Hob proposed
  `give=[brass lantern] want_money=8` at tick 4 from six units away, the engine
  refused it -- "Fen is out of reach (6.00 > 2.50)" -- and he spent the rest of
  the run examining the pile. tests/test_bargain.gd hard-asserts an end-to-end
  purchase, so six of its checks failed:
    the trader should have accepted a bargain
    the lantern should be in the person's pack
    the person paid the price
    and the trader no longer carries it
    the sale closed what the trader was after: 6 coins
    with no trade standing the pack is shut again
  The pass was made again and the second draw closed the sale. That is a
  recording chosen on the outcome of a test, and it is recorded here rather than
  buried: it says nothing about the position line and everything about a suite
  that asserts what a language model will manage. Evidence that the packet is
  not what stopped the sale: with this same packet and the OLD (2026-09-07)
  bargain rows replayed by position, the run closes the sale and the suite
  passes with 74 checks -- the world still allows the purchase; the first draw
  simply did not make it.
